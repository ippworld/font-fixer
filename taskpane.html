What to expect
  • The add-in replaces your slides with fixed copies. Undo (⌘Z) may not bring them back.
  • Save a copy first: File > Save As, with a new name.
  • If the result looks wrong, close the deck without saving and reopen the original. This works when AutoSave is off.
